Frequently Asked

How long does a project usually take?

expand_more

Most full website projects take around 4–8 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly content and feedback are provided. Single landing pages can often be completed faster.

What exactly is no‑code development?

expand_more

No‑code means I use modern visual platforms to design and build your site instead of traditional hand‑coding. You still get a high‑quality, custom website—but without needing a developer for every small change.

Do you handle branding and visuals as well?

expand_more

I provide art direction, layout, and on‑site visual identity for every project. If you already have a brand, I’ll work within it. If not, I’ll help shape a clear, consistent look and feel for your website.

Why do you focus on static websites?

expand_more

Static marketing sites are fast, secure, and easy to host. They’re ideal for serious brands that want a premium presence without the maintenance, plugin issues, and security risks of heavy CMS setups.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

expand_more

Yes. I build with user‑friendly tools so you can update text and images on your own. For larger structural changes or new pages, you can always bring me back in as needed.

Do you work with clients outside India?

expand_more

Yes. I work with clients in India and internationally. My process is built for remote collaboration using calls, Loom videos, and clear feedback rounds across time zones.

Are ecommerce websites included in these packages?

expand_more

The packages on this page are for static and marketing websites only. If you need ecommerce or a more complex system, we can discuss it on a discovery call and I’ll quote it separately.

What happens after I book a discovery call?

expand_more

On the call, we’ll look at your current situation, your goals for the website, and your budget and timeline. If we’re a good fit, I’ll outline a clear next step and a package that makes sense for you.
